## Overview & Company Profile

**Associated Alcohols & Breweries Ltd** is a prominent player in the alcoholic beverages sector in India. The company is listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) under the ticker **ASALCBR**. Founded in 1989 and headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, the company engages in the manufacturing, distillation, and bottling of a variety of liquor products.  
Key details include:  
- **Incorporation:** 1989  
- **Headquarters:** Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India  
- **Industry:** Alcoholic Beverages – Wineries & Distilleries  
- **Employees:** Approximately 567 (as per available profile data)  
- **Website:** [associatedalcohols.com](https://associatedalcohols.com)  



---

## Business Model & Products

The company operates through two primary segments:
- **Potable Alcohols:** Production of extra neutral alcohol along with Indian Made Indian Liquor (IMIL) and Indian Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL).  
- **Ethanol:** Manufacture of grain-based ethanol.

**Key Brands and Offerings:**
- **In-house brands:** Central Province Whisky, Titanium Triple Distilled Vodka, Bombay Special Whisky, Superman Fine Whisky, James Mc Gill Whisky, and Jamaican Magic Rum.
- **Franchise & Licensed Brands:** Includes internationally recognized names such as Black Dog, Captain Morgan, Smirnoff, VAT 69, Bagpiper, and White Mischief Vodka.

This diversified portfolio allows the company to serve both the domestic market and export markets effectively.

## SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- **Diverse Product Portfolio:** Robust lineup across multiple liquor categories including premium and mass-market brands.
- **Strong Market Presence:** Well-established in the domestic market with international exports.
- **Stable Promoter Holding:** Over 59% promoter stake suggests long-term alignment with shareholder interests.

### Weaknesses
- **Low Dividend Payout:** Dividend yields remain very low, which might deter income-focused investors.
- **Valuation Concerns:** High P/E ratios indicate that the stock might be trading at a premium.

### Opportunities
- **Expansion into Premium Segments:** Recent moves into premium spirits and ethanol production could boost future revenues.
- **Market Growth:** Favorable government policies and growing consumer trends in the alcohol industry offer upside potential.

### Threats
- **Regulatory Risks:** The alcohol industry is subject to strict regulations and taxation policies which could impact margins.
- **Market Competition:** Intense competition both from domestic players and international brands.

## Conclusion

Associated Alcohols & Breweries Ltd (ASALCBR) is a well-established player with a diversified product portfolio and strong market presence.
